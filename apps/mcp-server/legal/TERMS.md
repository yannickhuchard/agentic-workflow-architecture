## The service

The plugin lets an AI client validate, display and simulate documents of the open standard Agentic Workflow Architecture (AWA): workflows and customer journeys. It needs a client that supports MCP; the embedded views need a client that supports MCP Apps.

## Simulation only

Everything the service runs is a simulation. It never calls the tools, commands, services or AI models that a document names, and it never acts on a production system. A simulated run or journey shows how a document behaves with the outputs and events you supplied. It is not a forecast, a test of a real system, or evidence that a real process took place. Costs it reports are arithmetic on the rates and usage you supplied.

Validation checks a document against the AWA schema and its integrity rules. A valid document is well formed; it is not thereby correct, lawful or fit for your purpose.
