    2. Propose a top-up before 31 December to members below the Art. 111bis ceiling

07 Appendix

The EU AI Act and the record

Evidence, not a certification. This report is evidence computed from the execution journals and journey records listed in it. Every statement cites the records it rests on. It is not a certification, an audit opinion or a conformity assessment: it does not establish that any system, deployer or provider complies with the EU AI Act, the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 or any other framework. Whether these records meet an obligation depends on the system, its intended purpose, its risk classification and the applicable law, which this report does not assess.

Glossary

Art. 111bis LIR
The article of the Luxembourg income tax law under which premiums paid into a pension savings contract (prévoyance-vieillesse) are deductible, up to 4,500 EUR a year per taxpayer from tax year 2026, whatever the age. https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/p/prevoyance_vieillesse.html
Art. 111 LIR
The article under which life, death, accident, disability, sickness and civil liability insurance premiums are deductible, within 672 EUR a year per member of the household, shared between all those premiums. https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/c/cotis_prim.html
Art. 110 LIR
The article under which an employee's personal contributions to the employer's supplementary pension scheme are deductible, up to 1,200 EUR a year. https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/fiscalite/declaration-impot-decompte/depenses-deductibles/regimes-complementaire-pension-resident.html
Unit-linked fund
A fund in which the savings of a life insurance policy are invested in units whose value goes up and down, so that the policyholder, not the insurer, bears the investment risk. https://www.caa.lu/uploads/documents/files/LC26-1_FR.pdf
Key information document
The short document the EU PRIIPs regulation requires for each fund, stating its objective, its costs, its summary risk indicator from 1 to 7 and its performance scenarios. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/1286/oj
Summary risk indicator
The risk class of a fund on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 7, printed in its key information document; this example compares the weighted indicator of a choice with the highest one the risk profile allows. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2017/653/oj
Suitability assessment
The check, required by the Insurance Distribution Directive when an insurance-based investment product is sold with advice, that the product fits the customer's knowledge and experience, financial situation and objectives. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/97/oj
Demands and needs
The statement, made before any insurance contract is concluded, of what the customer wants the contract for and needs from it, so that only a consistent contract is proposed. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/97/oj
Risk profile
The class, from cautious to dynamic, that the employee's suitability answers place them in, with the highest summary risk indicator their choice of funds may reach. Decision table “Risk profile” in plan-invitation-and-advice.awa.json
Capacity for loss
How much of the savings the employee could lose without changing their way of life, part of the financial situation the suitability assessment asks about. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2017/2359/oj
Sustainability preferences
Whether, and how far, the employee wants the savings invested sustainably, which the suitability assessment must ask about. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2021/1257/oj
Model portfolio
A ready-made allocation of the plan's funds for each risk profile, which the employee may take instead of choosing funds one by one. adapters/mock-server.mjs, MODEL_PORTFOLIOS
Politically exposed person (PEP)
Someone who holds or held a prominent public function, such as a seat on the supervisory board of a state-owned enterprise, or a family member or close associate of one; a business relationship with them needs enhanced due diligence and senior management's approval. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/849/oj
Customer due diligence
Identifying the customer, screening them against the lists of politically exposed persons and sanctions, and understanding the source of their funds, before the relationship begins. https://www.caa.lu/en
CRS and FATCA self-certification
The declaration in which the employee states their countries of tax residence, their tax identification numbers and whether they are a US person, so that the insurer reports the policy to the right tax authorities. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/107/oj
Beneficiary clause
Who receives the savings if the employee dies before they are paid out: the standard clause (the spouse or partner, then the children, then the heirs), or named people with their shares. Savings Plan Subscription, “Review beneficiary clause”
Payroll deduction
The contribution the employer withholds from the salary each month and pays to the insurer, under the mandate the employee signed. Payroll Contributions workflow
Right to cancel
The 30 days after the employee is told the life insurance contract is concluded, in which they may cancel it; this plan then refunds every premium in full. https://www.caa.lu/uploads/documents/files/Loi_ContratAssurance_1997-07-27_coord_2025-11-25.pdf
Custodian bank
The bank, approved by the Commissariat aux Assurances, that holds the assets covering the policies, apart from the insurer's own assets. https://www.caa.lu/en
Triangle of security
The Luxembourg protection of life insurance savings resting on the insurer, the custodian bank that holds the covering assets apart, and the Commissariat aux Assurances that supervises both, with the policyholders' first-ranking claim on those assets (the super-privilege). https://www.caa.lu/en
Tax certificate
The yearly certificate in which the insurer states the premiums paid in the tax year and the article they fall under, for the employee's tax return. https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/fiscalite/declaration-impot-decompte/depenses-deductibles/contrat-prevoyance-resident.html
Top-up
A one-off payment on top of the monthly contribution; here it is collected with the December salary to reach the year's deduction ceiling. Year-End Tax, “Write top-up proposal”
Assimilation to a resident
The option a non-resident taxpayer with most of their income in Luxembourg has to be taxed as a resident, which gives access to deductions such as Art. 111bis. https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/a/assimil.html
Journey
The way one customer, order or other subject travels with the organisation, stage by stage, moved on by business events. §19
Stage
One part of a journey, with an owner and the time it is given. §19.2
Touchpoint
A moment where the customer and the organisation meet: a form, a call, a letter, a portal. §19.8
Mood
How the customer seems to feel on the journey's scale: stated by the customer, or inferred by a rule the journey declares. §19.9
Run
One execution of a workflow, recorded step by step in its journal. §5.1
Outcome contract
What must be true of a result, whatever route the agent took to it; a result that breaks it is tried again or refused. §11
Guardrail
A check on what goes into or comes out of an AI agent or a tool; when it finds something, it warns, redacts, blocks, asks for another attempt or hands the case to a person. §31
Separation of duties
A rule that stops the same principal from doing two steps that must have different hands, such as submitting a request and approving it. §30.3
Four eyes
A rule that a step needs the approval of two or more different people before the work goes on. §30.3
Service level
How long the work should take and the longest it may take; the run records a warning, then a breach. §23
Undo step
An activity that undoes the effect of an earlier one when the run fails, so that nothing is left half done. §13.6
Counterfactual replay
A recorded run run again with one thing changed, such as a contract or a rule, everything else held as recorded, and the two compared step by step. §20
Recommendation
A change to the journey or the work that a rule found in the record, shown with the numbers that fired it, the customers behind it, its owner and how its effect is known. View
Impact
How much a recommendation matters: the share of customers it concerns times how much it costs them, 3 for a journey lost, 2 for a stage or a promise outlasted or a fall of mood, 1 for friction. View
Effort
What a recommendation takes: 1 for a change of options, policy or a contract budget, 2 for a workflow change such as a timer or a touchpoint, 3 for new work for people. View
Right first time
The share of ended runs that completed with no failed step, no retry and no undo step. §21.4

Method
