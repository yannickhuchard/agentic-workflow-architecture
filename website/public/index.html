AWA

Agentic Workflow Architecture

An open standard for business workflows in which AI agents, people, robots and applications work side by side, with customer journeys on top. Fixed outcome, free strategy: AWA fixes what must be true of a result, records how it was reached, and refuses what it cannot verify.

MCP server

Validate, show and simulate AWA workflows and journeys from your AI assistant. Add this address as a custom connector, with no authentication:

https://agentic-workflow-architecture.web.app/mcp

Everything it runs is a simulation, and it keeps nothing between calls. The guide gives the steps for Claude, ChatGPT, Mistral, Cursor and Gemini CLI.
