# Code of Conduct

The AWA project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that address applies to every space of the project: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and every event or channel that represents the project.

## Our commitment

Everyone who takes part in the project commits to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community. We show empathy and kindness, we respect differing opinions, we give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, we take responsibility for our mistakes, and we focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, contact a maintainer listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md) privately. The maintainers review every report promptly and fairly, and they protect the privacy and the safety of the person who reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, chosen according to the impact of the behaviour.
