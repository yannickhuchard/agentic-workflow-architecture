# Valoria Life

Valoria Life is a fictional company, made up for AWA's examples. Its name, logo and colours belong to no real insurer.

## Overview

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Pages are light and quiet; one deep teal marks what a member can act on.

## Colors

- **Primary (#0E7C66):** deep teal, for buttons, the stage in view and bars.
- **Secondary (#C9A227):** a muted gold, for the logo only.
- **Surface (#F6F7FB):** a cool off-white page, with white cards.
- **On-surface (#0F172A):** near-black ink.

## Typography

Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for the body.

## Shapes

Soft corners: 8 px on buttons and boxes, 12 px on cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the teal for what a member can act on.
- Don't use the gold for text.
